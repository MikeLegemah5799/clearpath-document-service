1. Parties to this agreement

This Client Service Contract ("Agreement") is entered into between Clear Path Document Service, including its founder, staff and any authorized representatives ("Service Provider"), and the client named above ("Client"). By signing this Agreement, both parties agree to the terms below.

2. Fees & payment terms

The Client agrees to pay the service fee listed above. The following payment terms apply:

  • A non-refundable deposit of $50.00 is required at the time of booking to secure the appointment. This deposit is applied toward the total service fee.
  • The remaining balance is due upon completion of the document preparation, prior to delivery of completed documents.
  • Accepted payment methods: Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or cash.
  • A correction or amendment fee of $75.00 applies if changes are required after document completion because of inaccurate or incomplete information provided by the Client.
  • Additional follow-up, questions or continued assistance beyond the original scope of service are billed at $85.00 per hour.

3. Client responsibilities

  • The Client is solely responsible for the accuracy, completeness and truthfulness of all information provided to the Service Provider.
  • The Client must provide all required documents, identification and supporting materials in a timely manner.
  • Delays caused by the Client's failure to provide requested information may result in rescheduling or additional fees.
  • The Client is responsible for reviewing all completed documents before submission to any agency, court or institution.

4. Limitation of services & no legal advice

Important notice: Clear Path Document Service is a document preparation service only. Neither the company nor any of its staff, representatives or associates are attorneys or licensed to practice law. No person acting on behalf of Clear Path Document Service is authorized to provide legal advice, legal representation or legal counsel of any kind. The Service Provider makes no guarantee regarding the approval, acceptance or outcome of any document, application, petition or filing prepared under this Agreement. The Client is strongly encouraged to consult a licensed attorney for legal advice.

5. Refund policy

  • The $50.00 booking deposit is non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • If the Client cancels services after work has begun, fees for work completed to that point will be retained by the Service Provider.
  • No refunds are issued based on the outcome or result of any submitted document or application.

6. Confidentiality

The Service Provider agrees to keep all Client information strictly confidential and will not share, sell or disclose any personal information to any third party without the Client's written consent, except as required by law.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with written or verbal notice. In the event of termination, the Client is responsible for payment of all services rendered up to the date of termination. The non-refundable deposit will not be returned.

8. Agreement & signature

By signing below, the Client acknowledges that they have read, understood and agree to all terms in this Contract. The Client confirms that all information provided is accurate and that they understand Clear Path Document Service does not provide legal advice or guarantee any outcome.
