How it works

Three simple steps. You decide the pace.

1

Send the link or fill out the form

Use the form on this page, or ask the person who referred you to send it along. Tell us what kind of paperwork you are facing. Rough details are fine.

2

Connect

We will reach out by email or phone, offer a free 15-minute consultation, and explain what the paperwork involves and what information and documents you will need to gather.

3

Finish the paperwork

We help you organize your documents and complete each form, step by step, so it is ready for you to review. You make every decision and you keep control of your information.

This is administrative help, not legal advice. For legal questions, please speak with a licensed attorney.

Paperwork we help with

We focus on three areas of New York State paperwork. Every service has one set price, with no ranges.

NYS guardianship petitions and supporting documents

We help you understand what each guardianship form is asking, gather the information it needs, and complete the paperwork. We do not decide which type of guardianship is right for anyone. A licensed attorney can advise you on that.

  • Already started? Help continuing$150
  • Complex guardianship (multiple forms or parties)$600

SSI and SSDI applications and related paperwork

Applications, appeals and the paperwork that goes with them for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI). We help you understand the questions, gather what is needed and complete the forms.

  • Initial application$175
  • Appeal or reconsideration paperwork$125
  • Full package (application and supporting documents)$250

FMLA and NY Paid Family Leave forms

Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and New York Paid Family Leave forms look simple and still trip people up. We help you complete them accurately.

  • Single form$65
  • Full packet (multiple forms)$110

Booking and additional fees

These apply to every service:

  • Free 15-minute consultation
  • $50 booking deposit, non-refundable and applied toward your total
  • $75 correction or amendment fee when a completed document needs to be changed
  • $85 per hour for extra questions, additions or follow-up
