What is the difference between SSI and SSDI?

Both are Social Security Administration disability programs. SSDI (Social Security Disability Insurance) is based on your work history and the Social Security taxes you paid. SSI (Supplemental Security Income) is need-based and has limits on income and resources. Some people qualify for one, some for both. The Social Security Administration decides eligibility, and we help you complete the paperwork it asks for.

What is the difference between FMLA and New York Paid Family Leave?

FMLA is a federal law that can protect your job while you take unpaid leave for certain family or medical reasons, if you and your employer meet its requirements. New York Paid Family Leave is a state program that provides paid time off with job protection for reasons such as bonding with a new child or caring for a family member with a serious health condition. Many families complete forms for both. Eligibility depends on your situation, so confirm details with your employer, your leave carrier or a licensed attorney.

Do you tell me which type of guardianship I need?

No. Choosing a type of guardianship, deciding whether guardianship is right for someone, and explaining legal rights are legal questions. A licensed New York attorney or your local legal aid office can advise you. We help you understand and complete the forms once you know what you are filing.
