What is the difference between Article 81 and Article 17-A guardianship in New York?

In general terms, Article 81 of the New York Mental Hygiene Law is the process for adults who may be unable to manage their personal needs or property, and it is handled in the county Supreme Court. Article 17-A of the Surrogate's Court Procedure Act is a separate process for people with intellectual or developmental disabilities, handled in Surrogate's Court. Which one applies is a legal question. An attorney or the court clerk's office can tell you, and we help with the forms once you know.
